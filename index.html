About us

A local partner for importers and exporters in the GTA

Port 7 Customs Brokerage provides customs brokerage services to local businesses, including manufacturers, raw material handlers, and distributors of electronics, fashion and garments, as well as producers in the food industry. We help these companies move their shipments from overseas to their destinations safely, quickly and cost-effectively.
