How it works

From first call to final accounting.

A clear path for every shipment, so you always know where your goods are in the process.

Send your documents

Share your commercial invoice and shipment details by phone, email or our quote form.

We classify & file

Goods are classified under the Harmonized System and release data is sent to CBSA.

Your goods are released

With PARS, release information can be reviewed before your shipment reaches the border.

We close the file

Final accounting and any corrections are handled after release, and local trucking if you need it.
