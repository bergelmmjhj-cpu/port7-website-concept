About Port 7

The GTA's partner at the border.

A Richmond Hill customs broker serving the local businesses that bring goods in from overseas.

Who we are

We get shipments from overseas to their destination in a way that's safe, fast and cost-effective.

Port 7 Customs Brokerage is a service provider offering customs brokerage to local suppliers in the Greater Toronto Area: manufacturers, raw material handlers, distributors of electronics, fashion and garments, and producers in the food industry.

Who we serve

Built for the businesses that keep the GTA stocked.

Manufacturers

Imported parts, equipment and inputs cleared so production keeps running.

Raw material handlers

Bulk and raw material shipments classified and released correctly.

Electronics distributors

Overseas electronics shipments moved from the border to your warehouse.

Fashion & garment

Apparel and textile imports cleared in time for the season.

Food producers

Food-industry imports handled by a team that knows the paperwork.

Individuals

Personal effects clearance for people moving their belongings to Canada.

What we stand for

Three promises, every shipment.

Safe

Accurate classification and filings, so your goods arrive without surprises.

Fast

Electronic release and pre-arrival options to keep shipments moving.

Cost-effective

Correct duties the first time, plus corrections and drawback where you're owed money.
