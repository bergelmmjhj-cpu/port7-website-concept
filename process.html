Pre-Arrival Release System

Cleared before it reaches the border.

With PARS, your release information goes to the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) before your shipment arrives, so it can be reviewed in advance instead of when the truck pulls up.

Step by step

How a PARS shipment moves.

Who does what, from your supplier's invoice to goods at your door.

  • YouSend us your shipment details.
  • Port 7Prepares and files everything with CBSA.
  • Carrier & CBSAPresentation at the border and the release decision.
  1. You

    Send your shipment documents

    Share the commercial invoice and shipment details from your supplier as soon as they're available.

  2. Port 7

    Classify and prepare

    We classify your goods under the Harmonized System and prepare the release information CBSA needs.

  3. Port 7

    Submit PARS before arrival

    The release request is transmitted electronically to CBSA ahead of time, so it can be reviewed before the shipment reaches the border.

  4. Carrier

    The shipment arrives

    The PARS reference travels with the carrier's paperwork and links the truck to the release information already on file.

  5. CBSA

    Release decision

    CBSA makes the release decision at the border. Goods may still be selected for examination.

  6. Port 7

    Final accounting and delivery

    We complete the B3 final accounting and can arrange local trucking to your door.

Why it matters

Paperwork done before, not at, the border.

Leaving it to arrival

  1. Shipment reaches the border
  2. Release information is reviewed then
  3. Any questions are dealt with while the goods wait

With PARS

  1. Release information is sent ahead of time
  2. CBSA can review it before the shipment arrives
  3. Issues can be sorted out before the truck gets to the border

Ask about your shipment

What does PARS stand for?

Pre-Arrival Release System. It lets release information be submitted to CBSA before goods arrive in Canada.

Which shipments use PARS?

PARS is commonly used for shipments that arrive by truck. Talk to us about which release option fits your shipment.

What do I need to send?

Start with the commercial invoice and shipment details from your supplier. We'll tell you if anything else is needed.

Does PARS guarantee my goods will be released?

No. CBSA always makes the release decision and can select any shipment for examination. PARS means the information is ready for review ahead of time.

What happens after release?

We complete the B3 final accounting, handle any later corrections, and can arrange local trucking to your door.
