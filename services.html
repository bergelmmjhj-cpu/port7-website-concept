Services

Everything your shipment needs at the border.

Eighteen services across four stages: getting set up to import, clearing your goods, accounting after release, and moving exports and freight.

01 · Getting set up 02 · Customs clearance 03 · Accounting & compliance 04 · Exports & trucking
01

Getting set up

The registrations your business needs before its first shipment crosses the border.

Incorporation

Help getting your company formally set up, so you're ready to trade.

Import / export registration

Registering your business number for import and export activity so your goods can be cleared under your name.

Non-Resident Importer registration

For companies based outside Canada that want to import into Canada as the importer of record.

02

Customs clearance

Getting your goods released by the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) quickly and correctly.

Commercial customs clearance

End-to-end clearance of commercial shipments arriving from overseas.

Personal effects customs clearance

Clearing household goods and personal belongings for individuals moving to Canada.

EDI release

Release information sent to CBSA electronically, for faster and paperless processing.

PARS

Pre-Arrival Release System

Release data is submitted before your goods arrive, so CBSA can review it in advance. How PARS works

ACI

Advance Commercial Information

Shipment and cargo information provided to CBSA ahead of arrival, as Canadian rules require.

e-Manifest

Electronic cargo and conveyance information filed in advance for shipments arriving by truck.

03

Accounting & compliance

The paperwork after release: duties, corrections and refunds, done right.

B3

B3 final accounting

Final accounting for your imported goods, including the duties and taxes owed.

B2

B2 correction

Adjustment requests to correct previously accounted imports and recover overpaid amounts where eligible.

RMD

RMD correction

Corrections to Release on Minimum Documentation (RMD) transactions.

HS

Harmonized System code classification

Assigning the correct tariff classification to your goods, which determines the duty you pay.

Duty drawback

Claiming back duties paid on imported goods that are later exported, where they qualify.

CUSMA

CUSMA (formerly NAFTA) certificate

Certificate of origin paperwork to support preferential duty treatment for qualifying North American goods.

04

Exports & trucking

Reporting goods leaving Canada, and moving freight locally.

Export declaration

Reporting your outbound shipments to CBSA when an export declaration is required.

B13

B13 filing

Preparing and filing B13A export declarations on your behalf.

Local trucking

Local transport to get your cleared goods from the border or terminal to your door.
